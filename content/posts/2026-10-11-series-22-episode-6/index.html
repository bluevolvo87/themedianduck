Gamble’s Gamble

End-of-Series Ranking Distributions

Figure 8: Series Ranking Distribution: Using Data up to Episode 5 (left), and up to Episode 6 (right).

The marginal distributions of each contestants series placement is presented in Figure 8.

The most probable series ranking placement for each contestant are:

  • Chloe: 1st with 99.03% probability; previously 1st with 96.22%.
  • Isy: 3rd with 42.76% chance; previously 4th with 59.43%.
  • Matt: 4th with 54.00% probability; previously 3rd with 54.11%.
  • Nina: 2nd with 71.42% chance; previously 2nd with 78.92%.
  • Richard: 5th with 98.08% probability; previously 5th with 98.19%.

Chloe and Richard’s distribution have retained the same peaks and centers; it is pretty cemented (and reflected with near certain probability) that they will place 1st and 5th respectively. This does assume that their historic performance is an accurate representation of their future performance in the remaining episodes of the series.

Nina’s distribution still remains the same shape, but is noticeably less peaked on placing 2nd. This is a reflection of Nina’s potential three-way tango with Isy and Matt, as they tango between finishing 2nd, 3rd and 4th for the series, and the uncertainty of how this will pan out in reality.

Isy and Matt’s distributions have changed the most with their centers and peaks shifting to their current standings in the series (3rd and 4th respectively). Their distributions are noticeably the most flat compared to other cast members, a reflection of how close they are in the series standings and the associated uncertainty of how this will pan out by the end of the series.
