Gamble’s Gamble

End-of-Series Ranking Distributions

Figure 10: Series Ranking Distribution: Using Data up to Episode 7 (left), and up to Episode 8 (right).

The marginal distributions of each contestants series placement is presented in Figure 10. The most probable series ranking placement for each contestant are:

  • Chloe: 1st with 95.81% probability; previously 1st with 99.96%.
  • Isy: 3rd with 87.77% chance; previously 3rd with 49.76%.
  • Matt: 4th with 65.03% probability; previously 4th with 49.66%.
  • Nina: 2nd with 91.04% chance; previously 2nd with 90.89%.
  • Richard: 5th with 67.23% probability; previously 5th with 93.37%.

Other observations:

  • Chloe and Nina’s marginal distributions have remained the same shape, although there are some slight changes to the amount of probability mass associated with their most probable ranking outcome.
    • Chloe has slightly less probability associated with being crowned the series champion, a result of her previous 19 point lead decreasing after Episode 8.
    • Nina has slightly more probability associated with placing 2nd in the series due to her gaining a 9 point lead above 3rd place. We also see a 5% increase in probability associated with being crowned series champion, a result of her closing the gap to the current series frontrunner Chloe.
  • Isy’s distribution has become incredibly centered and peaked on placing 3rd in the series.
    • 12.23% probability is assigned to Isy NOT coming 3rd in the series.
    • Such increased peakedness is likely due to her not having to compete for 3rd place with Matt, and Nina gaining a 9 point lead after Episode 8.
  • Matt’s distribution is now peaked and skewed towards the lower end of the rankings (4th and 5th place).
    • There is just under 30% probability that Matt could slip into 5th place for the series, which is a noticeable increase compared to prior weeks’ distributions.
  • Richard’s distribution continues to be skewed towards the lower rankings (4th and 5th place); but there is a 30% glimmer of hope that he doesn’t end as the series 22 loser.
    • His improvement in the last couple of episodes is definitely reflected in his marginal distributions for end of series rankings.
