What Have We Learnt Today?

We’ve learnt that, all things considered, alas, I am not Fern Brady.

I also recently finished Fern Brady’s memoir “Strong Female Character”, which I was thoroughly entertained and educated by. She details her late in life diagnosis as an autistic women and not fitting the typical stereotypes associated with people with autism (there are different indicators for men and women).

Here are some useful links if you want more information on the book:

  • Autistic Review,
  • Goodreads review,
  • Amazon Product Detail Page (please support local independent bookstores though).

She does briefly mention how her obsession with learning to place Mozart Piano Sonatas (of which Rondo Alla Turca does belong to as it is the 3rd movement of Piano Sonata No. 11 in A major, K. 331 / 300i) was a potential indicator to autism, and her time on Taskmaster (her meltdowns can be triggered by odd lighting setups, why she frequently proclaimed that she was tired).
