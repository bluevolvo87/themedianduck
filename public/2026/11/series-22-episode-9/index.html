End-of-Series Ranking Distributions

Figure 8: Series Ranking Distribution: Using Data up to Episode 8 (left), and up to Episode 9 (right).

The marginal distributions of each contestants series placement is presented in Figure 8. The most probable series ranking placement for each contestant are:

  • Chloe: 1st with 99.62% probability; previously 1st with 95.81%.
  • Isy: 3rd with 93.06% chance; previously 3rd with 87.77%.
  • Matt: 4th with 84.30% probability; previously 4th with 65.03%.
  • Nina: 2nd with 95.04% chance; previously 2nd with 91.04%.
  • Richard: 5th with 81.28% probability; previously 5th with 67.23%.

The distributions are in line with the current series standings. Compared to last week’s distributions, they have all retained the same shape (peaks and centers), with more probability mass (as in certainty) on the peaks (most probable outcome).

Going into the series finale, the main jeopardy (as in uncertainty) will lie on how the men will place at the bottom of the series. That is whether Matt will place 4th and Richard in 5th, or Richard will place 4th and Matt in 5th.

Joint Cast Ranking Distributions

Figure 10: Joint Distribution of Cast Series Ranking: Using Data up to Episode 8 (left), and up to Episode 9 (right).

From Figure 10, the joint cast ranking distribution, the most probable cast ranking is [1st: Chloe, 2nd: Nina, 3rd: Isy, 4th: Matt, 5th: Richard] with roughly 74% chance of occurring. This is the same most probable combination following Episodes 6 to 8, and also the current series standings at the end of Episode 9.

The second most probable cast ranking combination with just over 15% chance of occurring has Matt and Richard’s series ranking reversed (Matt in 5th, Richard in 5th).

Other cast ranking combinations with just under 5% probability occurring feature a tie occurring between Matt and Richard (3rd most probable), and Isy overtaking Nina into 2nd place.

The 5th most probable series ranking combination with just under 2.5% chance occurring has Isy and Nina tying for 2nd place.

What Have We Learnt Today?

We’ve learnt that:

  • Isy won her 2nd episode for the series!
    • Isy has been very uniform with her episode placements in the series. She has placed 1st, 3rd, 4th and 5th exactly twice in the series so far. If she can place 2nd in the series finale, this would be good for those with OCD tendencies.
  • Chloe and Nina have now achieved the straight flush in episode placement feat.
    • Only Matt has yet to achieve this feat, in addition to not winning an episode.
  • The outcome of the series is somewhat set in stone. The most probable cast ranking is [1st: Chloe, 2nd: Nina, 3rd: Isy, 4th: Matt, 5th: Richard] with 74% chance of occurring.
    • The main area of uncertainty is how the bottom of the series leaderboard will pan out in reality. There is just slightly less than 15% chance that Richard will overtake Matt into 4th place.
    • There is just under 5% chance that Matt and Richard will end the series in a tie on 4th place.
    • There is also just under 5% chance that Isy will overthrow Nina and finish in 2nd place.
  • My relocation activity has definitely ramped up, as indicated by how late I am publishing this post.
