Episode Recap

In Episode 3, we saw:

  • The introduction of the BOSNAC form, Big or Small Noughts and Crosses. In the US, this would be called BOSTTT for Big or Small Tic-Tac-Toe, or if we are taking into account McDonald portion sizing LORTTT for Large or Regular Tic-Tac-Toe.
    • We can add this to the growling list of Taskmaster games including Front-ham, Human war boats and Snakes & Steps.
  • The toilet rolls of Hanoi were unveiled as the 8th Wonder of the World. However, it wasn’t clear if toilet rolls could only be moved onto poles in which the highest toilet roll was of the same colour.
  • Sheep need to be sheared frequently so that they don’t fall over from their own weight (and presumably die from falling over).
  • Gleeking was introduced to the nation, much to annoyance of parents and teachers.
    • I remember Edward Turk in my form group being able to do this with ease, and being equally revolted and amazed by it.
  • There is a curse on the Taskmaster house! The gargoyle is actually Little Alex Horne who presumably sets silly meaningless tasks for a group of comedians who visit the house (and are chosen by them)
    • Pedants will also remark that it is a grotesque rather than a gargoyle; a gargoyle has an actual architecteral purpose in that it is a waterspout which drains water away from a building, whilst a grotesque is purely decorative.

Figure 3: Episode Placement Distribution for each Contestant

From our Episode Performance Report Card (Figure 2 and Distribution of Episode Placements (Figure 3):

  • Chloe wins her second episode of the series! She achieved 21 points in this episode to secure her win.
    • This also the highest episode points in the series so far, and coincidentally, Chloe has also achieved the lowest episode points in the series.
    • Chloe’s 21 points is also the first time in this series that we have exceed the 20 point threshold for an episode.
    • Chloe’s high episode score and episode win no doubt benefited from the disqualification of Richard, Isy and Nina in the live studio task. Chloe would have ended up in 3rd place in the task had the disqualification of Richard and Isy had not occurred (and secured 19 points for the episode). However, Richard and Isy were clearly breaking the rule with at least 2 toilet rolls off the poles so their disqualification is justified.
  • Nina ends up in 2nd place for the third time for a row. She is very much always the bridesmaid and never the bride.
    • Nina’s episode score of 17 points is graded B+; it is not quite as good as Episode 1, but not as disastrous as her performance in Episode 2.
    • Nina could have won this episode, or at least got to the second tiebreaker of the series, if she didn’t get disqualified in the live task. She would have finished in 2nd place in the task, presumably earning 4 points1 which would have given her 21 points and matching Chloe’s 21 points.
    • Nina’s disqualification was a bitter pill to swallow; she wasn’t blatantly cheating like Richard and Isy and only had a maximum of 2 toilet rolls off the poles. It’s also because I have a soft spot for Nina, particularly as I’ve delved into her body of work.
  • Matt ended the episode in 3rd place with 13 points. This is his worse episode to date and thus graded D.
    • His flight attempt task certainly did not help his score.
  • Richard also fared similarly in his episode grade. He was graded a D, achieving his worst episode score to date with 11 points and finishing in 4th place for Episode 3.
  • Isy continues her current track record of being the weakest contestant amongst the series 22 cast. She ended up in 5th place, only earning 9 points in this episode and having her worst episode to date.
    • Her poor performance in the prize task and disqualification in the live task certainly do not help.

What Have We Learnt Today?

We’ve learnt that:

  • Sheeps do need to be sheared on a frequent basis, otherwise they will fall over (and presumably die) from the excess wool they are carrying around.
  • Chloe wins her second episode of the series, and the highest episode score of the series so far.
    • We have officially crossed into 20+ episode points territory for this series; Chloe is the first to achieve this feat.
  • Nina has always been the bridesmaid but never the bride; she has placed 2nd place in all 3 episodes.
  • But despite Nina’s 2nd place dominance, she is leading the series with 49 points!
    • However, she cannot be too comfortable as Chloe is hot on her boot heels with 48 points!
  • This 1 point series lead also doesn’t translate immediately into the most probable joint cast ranking.
    • The most probable cast combination is [1st: Chloe, 2nd: Nina, 3rd: Matt, 4th: Richard, 5th: Isy] with 20.82% chance.
    • However, there is an near equally probable combination in which Nina does succeed in being crowned the series champion.
  • The game of BOSNAC was introduced to the nation and will no doubt be sweeping that nation.
    • This game will have to be called LORTTT when it makes its way to the American audience, for reasons involving sizing conversions and naming conventions associated with a popular game.
