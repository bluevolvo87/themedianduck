For Series 22, I (your faithful Pacific Northwest Representative for Taskmaster UK), will be in the midst of preparing to permanently relocate back to the UK mothership by the end of 2026. Sadly, my return is not relocated for Taskmaster purposes (at the moment), but if anyone in the Taskmaster community would like to collaborate once I am physically back in my homeland, please do reach out!


As relocation is my main priority, your weekly commentary is liking going to be more less in depth (and humourous) than prior series. There will also be fewer instances of new innovative analysis and modelling approaches.

Episode Recap

The episode 1 featured:

  • The shed now having a tunnel built in which led not quite to Narnia, but to a Greek themed gazebo.
    • Whether this is a permanent area, or a temporary area that only featured in the golden apple task is unknown. Time will tell!
  • For the second series in a row, the teams were introduced in this premiere episode for the series. The teams are:
    • Isy Suttie and Nina Conti.
    • CHloe Petts, Matt Lucas and Richard Ayoade.
  • The introduction of the teams in Episode 1 is always a surprise since we typically have yet to fully digest each contestant as an individual. It worked a treat in Series 21 though; He’s so famous!
  • The teams themselves are quite the surprise, and no doubt due to availability and scheduling.
    • The main surprise comes from assigning Chloe and Richard to the same team. Prior to the episode, I suspected with relatively low probability, that they would be on the same team.
    • Ultimately, it shows how little I know in how the Taskmaster universe works.
    • I am excited by the team of Isy and Nina; it is almost like a mumsy night out. I did feel that they were at a significant disadvantage during the team task; disassembling and reassembling a bed is easier with more people!
  • This episode featured the musical talents of our contestants through the “Sing a passionate duet to yourself.”, and showcased that this a very musical cast.
    • All contestants attempts featured them playing their instruments to a reasonably competent degree. Only Nina was the one who showed some real hesistation and stage fright; she was making up the lyrics to her song as she went along though.
  • Richard Ayoade has already delievered his awkward, disruptive humour.
    • I suspect that this will continue throughout the series, and what fans are expecting from him and the show.
    • It was slightly disappointing that his we was extremely low effort and energy in the live studio task. I know that this is his style of comedy, but he could try at least bit harder and show some respect for the Taskmaster!

What Have We Learnt Today?

We’ve learnt that:

  • Daddy’s Back! Series 22 of UK Taskmaster has got off to a fine start.
  • Chloe Petts won the first episode of the series with 19 points. However, Nina and Matt could have easily claimed this title if the live studio task panned out slightly differently.
  • We also saw the teams for this series being established in this debut episode. The teams are [Nina, Isy] and [Chloe, Matt, Richard].
  • This is an extremely musically capable cast! They all appear to play musical instruments to a reasonable standard.
  • Richard Ayoade is already satisfying his awkward, disruptive quota.
  • Currently, our prediction of how the series will pan out after 10 episode is [1st: Chloe, 2nd: Nina, 3rd: Matt, 4th: Richard, 5th: Isy] with 47.24% probability.
  • We expect our distributions, predictions and inferences to change as the series progresses, even substantially at times!
    • In fact, we even expect the distributions to be quite volatile (changing a lot) in the first half of the series, from episode to episode, due to limited data (task attempts available).
    • They may even be inaccurate in the first half of the series!
