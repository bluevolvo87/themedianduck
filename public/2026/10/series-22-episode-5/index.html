From the episode performance report card (Figure 2), and the episode placement distribution (Figure 3):

  • Nina had a disastrous episode; she only scored 8 points over the episode and placing 5th in the episode.

    • Matt had a very good episode, scoring 16 points and landing in 2nd place.

    • Richard had a very bad episode, matching his existing lowest episode score of 9 points. He placed 4th in the episode by the end.
  • We can also see from Figure 3 that no cast member has placed in all 5 possible positions for an episode yet.
    • Isy is the nearest to accomplishing this straight flush, only requiring a 2nd place episode finish to achieve this feat.
    • Nina is the furthest away from accomplishing this feat. Time to get her boots on if she wants to achieve this by the end of the series.

From the task attempts distribution table (Figure 5), a potential consolation “prize” for Nina’s terrible episode performance is that she incurred the first active penalisation of the series (that is negative points were awarded). Series Scoreboard Tracker

Figure 7: Series Scoreboard Tracker

As indicated in our trusted series tracker (Figure 7):

  • Nina’s disastrous episode 5 means that she has lost her series head and dropped into 2nd place with 78 points.
    • As much as I have developed a new love for Nina, I am genuinely surprised that Nina’s has performed well in the series so far (see her not so stellar performance on House of Games).

But I would like to be proven wrong of course!

    • This 10 point lead was no doubt made possible by the live studio task. Saying that if Chloe had not won the live studio task, and Nina had not pulled the negative point sausage (or the snake), Chloe would still have 4 point series lead, which really indicates how badly Nina did in this episode.
  • Isy lost her 3rd series place to Matt; Matt is now in 3rd place (72 points) and Isy is closely behind in 4th place with 71 points.
    • I suspect that there will be a constant battle between these two contestants for 3rd and 4th place going forward. Nina may even join them…
  • Richard has pretty much cemented himself as the loser of the series, or as some people title it “The People’s Champion”.
    • A 15 point difference to 4th place is quite a significant gap, but not entirely impossible to overcome (see Joel Dommett from Series 21).

What Have We Learnt Today?

We’ve learnt that:

  • Chloe won her 3rd episode of the series and has now become the series leader with 88 points. She has a 10 point series leader over the 2nd placer Nina.
  • Nina’s fall from series leader position is following a disastrous Episode 5 in which she only accumulated 8 points, finished in 5th place. This was her worse episode to date.
    • Her 4 episode run of being always the bridesmaid, never the bride (she placed 2nd place in the first 4 episodes), came to an end, and now she is just the hired wedding singer.
    • She also incurred the first negative points for a task in the series from the absurd luck based live studio task.
  • There’s likely going to be a brawl between Matt and Isy for 3rd and 4th place for the series.
    • I have feeling that Nina may also join this brawl…
  • Richard has pretty much cemented himself as this series’ people champion (better known as loser). But never say never, particularly since we have 5 more episodes in the series and Richard may pull something out from his bag.
  • Isy just needs to place 2nd in an episode in order to achieve a straight flush with regards to episode placements.
  • We were introduced to the delightful low voiced character of Ron, who will help us fend of Isy’s scary witch character.
